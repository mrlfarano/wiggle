Wiggle

Record once, get a polished video automatically.

A professional screen recorder for Windows 11 that automatically applies auto-zoom, smooth cursor motion, and beautiful framing. No editing required — your raw recordings export as finished-looking videos.

⬇️ Download v0.1.0 📦 View on GitHub

Everything just works

Wiggle handles the polish so you don't have to.

🎯

Auto Cursor Zoom

Wiggle watches your cursor and automatically zooms in on clicks, drags, and pauses. Smooth ease-in/out transitions make every action feel intentional.

✨

Smooth Cursor Motion

Shaky mouse movement? Gone. Centripetal Catmull-Rom spline interpolation transforms jittery cursor paths into buttery-smooth curves. Click positions stay exact.

🎨

Visual Styling

Backgrounds, padding, drop shadows, borders. Make your recording look like a product demo, not a raw screen grab.

  • Custom background colors
  • Configurable padding & shadows
  • Border effects
🔊

Audio That Sounds Clean

Built-in noise gate, normalization, and multi-track mixing. Your microphone and system audio come through clear and balanced.

  • Noise gate with attack/release
  • Peak & RMS normalization
  • Mic + system audio mixing
📹

Webcam PiP

Overlay your webcam as a picture-in-picture. Viewers see your face alongside the action.

⚡

Motion Blur

A natural motion trail follows your cursor based on its velocity. Configurable intensity — from subtle to dramatic.

⌨️

Keyboard Shortcut Display

Show pressed keys as stylish keycap overlays. Perfect for tutorials and demos where shortcuts matter.

📐

Aspect Ratio Modes

One-click switching between 16:9 landscape and 9:16 portrait. All zooms auto-adjust when you change formats.

  • TikTok / Reels / Shorts presets
  • Square (1:1)
  • Auto zoom reframing
📝

Timeline Editor

Trim, cut, and fine-tune zoom keyframes. Drag to adjust timing, scrub the playhead, speed up or slow down sections.

  • Trim & cut segments
  • Drag-to-adjust zoom keyframes
  • Speed ramp (2x / 0.5x)

Get started in 60 seconds

No video editing experience needed.

# Download the installer (recommended) # → Wiggle-Setup-v0.1.0.exe (~58MB) # → Run it, find Wiggle in Start Menu # Or build from source: git clone https://github.com/mrlfarano/wiggle.git cd wiggle dotnet build ScreenStudio.sln -c Release
1️⃣

Press Record

The floating overlay stays out of your way. Choose full screen, a window, or a region. Toggle mic, audio, and webcam.

2️⃣

Present

Just do your thing. Wiggle captures everything — cursor, clicks, audio — and logs it with high-precision timestamps.

3️⃣

Press Stop → Export

The preview already shows auto-applied zooms and smooth cursor. Tweak if you want, then export to MP4, GIF, or WebM.
